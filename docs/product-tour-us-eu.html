The workflow

Coffee to closed books in six steps

This is the actual sequence, running live. It plays on its own — or click any step to jump.

01

Open to today

Net for the month, money in against spending, and the week beside it. No login screen, no sync spinner.

02

Tap the amount

One field, big keypad, quick amounts for the numbers you type most. Groceries, gas, a $6 latte.

03

Say how you paid

Card, Apple Pay, Venmo, Zelle, cash — the real methods for your country, not a generic list.

04

Saved, and the total moves

The entry lands and your remaining budget updates on the spot. Four seconds, start to finish.

05

Bills come to you

Rent, utilities, subscriptions — each on its own schedule. One tap confirms, or edit if the amount changed.

06

Know what's left

Left to spend before payday, where it went, and the categories quietly eating the month.
