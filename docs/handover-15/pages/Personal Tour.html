The workflow

From a $6 coffee to a month you can explain

Most apps start by asking for your bank password, then guess what your transactions were and tell you three days late. This starts from how you actually spend, and asks you almost nothing. It plays on its own — or click any step to jump.

01

Open to today

Net for the month, money in against spending, and the week beside it. No login screen, no sync spinner.

02

Pin what you buy every week

Your quick list keeps starred shortcuts, ones you added, and suggestions for your profile — each with its usual amount. Income, expense and asset picks get their own tab.

03

Tap the amount

One field, big keypad, quick amounts for the numbers you type most. Groceries, gas, a $6 latte.

04

Say how you paid

Card, Apple Pay, Venmo, Zelle, cash — the real methods for your country, not a generic list.

05

Saved, and the total moves

The entry lands and your remaining budget updates on the spot. Four seconds, start to finish.

06

Set it once, it repeats

Rent, utilities, subscriptions and your paycheck live on the Recurring tab, grouped into this week and later this month. Remind or auto-log, skip one, or pause it.

07

Bills come to you

On the day something is due it shows up on top. One tap confirms, or edit if the amount changed.

08

Know what's left

Left to spend before payday, where it went, and the categories quietly eating the month.

Why not just link your bank

Four things aggregators can't do for you

Bank-linking apps solved data entry and created four new problems. Each of these is a deliberate choice here, not a missing feature.

lock_openThey need your bank login
check_circleYou log it, or import a file yourself

Nothing to breach, nothing to revoke, no re-authenticating every 90 days. Switch on device-only mode and every entry stays off our servers entirely.

money_offThey can't see cash
check_circleCash is a wallet like any other

Set an opening balance and every entry moves the right wallet — cash, card, checking, Venmo, even a gift card. Aggregators leave a hole where your cash spending was.

event_busyThey tell you on the 30th
check_circleA pace mark tells you on the 12th

Every budget category carries a tick showing where you should be today, and says ahead, behind, or over by how much — while you can still do something about it.

card_giftcardThey ignore money you prepaid
check_circleGift cards you can spend from

A gift card is a real balance that counts down as you use it, warns you 30 days before expiry, and flags a low balance. Most apps never knew it existed.
